Digital Marketing & Strategy
Ek full-service Digital Marketing Agency ko hire karne ka kya fayda hai? ➕
Ek hi jagah se aapko branding, website, social media, ads, aur SEO mil jata hai. Isse aapki brand identity consistent rehti hai aur alag-alag freelancers se coordination ka time bachta hai. JR Digital Studio aapka complete digital growth partner hai.
Mere local business ko Digital Marketing ki kyu zarurat hai? ➕
Aaj ke time me 90% log Google ya Instagram par check karne ke baad hi kisi shop ya service par aate hain. Agar aap online visible nahi hain, toh aap apne competitors ko apne daily customers de rahe hain.
Paid Advertising (Meta & Google Ads)
Google Ads aur Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads mein kya fark hai? ➕
Google Ads (Search Ads) intent-based hote hain—jab log "Digital Agency in Mehsana" search karein, tab aapka ad dikhega. Meta Ads interest-based hote hain, jo target audience (jaise local youngsters ya business owners) ki feed mein brand awareness aur direct leads generate karte hain.
Lead Generation ke liye kaun sa ad platform sabse best hai? ➕
Dono powerful hain, lekin B2C (Customer) services ke liye Meta Ads bohot jaldi ROI dete hain, aur B2B ya high-ticket services ke liye Google Ads aur LinkedIn Marketing best rehti hai. Hum aapke business ke hisab se strategy banate hain.
Ads run karne ke liye minimum budget kitna hona chahiye? ➕
Aap daily ₹500 se ₹1000 ke budget se ad campaign start kar sakte hain. Hum ise optimize karke cost-per-lead (CPL) kam karne par focus karte hain taaki aapka ROI maximize ho.
Social Media Management & Content Creation
Social Media Management mein kya kya services milti hain? ➕
Isme hum 30-day content calendar plan karte hain. Carousel posts design, caption/script writing, hashtag research, competitor analysis, aur daily account handling shamil hoti hai. Aapko bas apna business handle karna hai, baki online growth hamari zimmedari.
Content Creation kyu zaroori hai? ➕
Sirf product ki photo dalne se ab business nahi badhta. Value-driven content (tips, behind the scenes, case studies) se audience ka trust build hota hai, jo followers ko paying customers mein convert karta hai.
Video Editing & Instagram Reels
Kya aap Instagram Reels aur YouTube shorts edit karte hain? ➕
Haan! Hum highly engaging, fast-paced video editing karte hain jisme trendy text animations, sound effects, B-rolls, aur color grading shamil hoti hai taaki video viral ho sake.
Agar main video shoot nahi kar sakta, toh kya aap video banate hain? ➕
Haan, hum stock footage, high-quality graphics, aur AI voiceovers ka use karke bhi premium faceless reels aur promotional videos create karte hain.
Logo Design, Brochures & Graphic Design
Brand Identity aur Logo Design mein aap kya deliver karte hain? ➕
Hum aapke brand ki aatma ko samajhkar premium logo concepts (Typography, Minimalist, ya Mascot) design karte hain. Final files sabhi formats (SVG, PNG, PDF) aur color codes (e.g. Blue & White theme) ke sath di jati hain.
Kya aap print-ready Pamphlets aur Brochures design karte hain? ➕
Bilkul! Hum offline marketing ke liye high-conversion A4 pamphlets, company profile brochures, business cards, aur hoardings/banners design karte hain (jaise local battery shops, cab services aadi ke liye).
Kya aap 3D floor plans aur interior designs banate hain? ➕
Haan, architects aur real estate builders ke liye hum ultra-realistic 3D architectural visualization, isometric views, aur interior design layouts provide karte hain.
Website Development & SEO
Custom Web Design aur templates mein kya fark hai? ➕
Templates dheeme hote hain aur sabhi ke liye ek jaise lagte hain. JR Digital Studio custom-coded, ultra-fast, aur SEO-optimized websites banata hai (jaise ye website jo aap dekh rahe hain), jisse conversion rates badhte hain.
Kya SEO me pehle page par rank karne ki guarantee hoti hai? ➕
SEO Google ke algorithm par depend karta hai, isliye overnight guarantee koi nahi de sakta. Lekin humari solid On-page SEO, GEO (AI Search Optimization), aur local content strategies ke zariye 3-6 mahine me aapka business top rankings hasil karne lagta hai.
Kya aap website ki complete hosting manage karte hain? ➕
Haan, domain purchase se lekar secure hosting (SSL Certificates, GitHub pages, GoDaddy DNS setup), hum end-to-end IT setup aur maintenance karte hain.
